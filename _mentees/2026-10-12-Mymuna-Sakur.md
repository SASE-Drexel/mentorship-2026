### About

I'm a second-year Computer and Electrical Engineering student and a first-generation college student. I'm especially interested in the hardware side of computers and learning more about how they work. I would describe myself as introverted, but lately I've been trying to step out of my comfort zone more by meeting new people and trying new things. I love learning new things and I'm always open to new experiences!

### Interests

Some of my interests include playing video games, badminton, volleyball, exploring new places, hanging out with friends and family, and trying new foods.

### Career Goals

My goal is to work with computer hardware in the future, since that's the area of computer engineering I'm most interested in. I also want to learn more about software and gain experience through internships to explore different career opportunities.

### What I’m Looking For in a Mentor

I'm looking for someone who I can comfortably talk to and ask questions without feeling nervous or judged. I'd love to have a mentor who can share their own experiences, give me advice about co-ops and classes, and help me figure out what direction I want to take. More than anything, I'm hoping to build a genuine connection with someone who is supportive and easy to talk to.
