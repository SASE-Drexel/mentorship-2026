Arts & crafts, food, photography, collecting blindboxes and trinkets, ice skating, and community organizing!

I hope to work with children as either a psychologist or therapist. Specifically I want to help children who have faced adversity. I also hope to help research and build mental health resource accessibility.

### What I’m Looking For in a Mentor

Someone who I can go to to talk about everything or if I need support. I would love a mentor who is also interested in going into the health field or has similar interests as me. I hope my mentor will share their experiences and stories and give me advice and support. And of course, someone who I can go on side quests with and eat with! 😋
